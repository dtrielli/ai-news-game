AI News Game

An educational game about how AI choices in journalism can affect audience reach and trust. Players choose Human, AI, or Human + AI for newsroom tasks, then discuss the results.

The facilitator website supports pen-and-paper play for up to eight players. College and high-school guides offer story introductions, discussion prompts, a task glossary, automatic calculations, and a closing reflection.
