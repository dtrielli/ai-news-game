Three-day newsroom simulation

Decide how the news gets made.

For every story, complete the tasks at the Reporting, Editing, Packaging, and Audience desks.

Assign each task to Human, AI, or Human + AI (verified automation). There is no clock, and every choice can be revised before publication.

Your progress is saved only in this browser so you can resume later. Nothing is transmitted or collected.

Instructions and scoring

How to play

Across three days, assign all 20 newsroom tasks for each of three stories. Choose Human, AI, or Human + AI. You may revise any assignment until you finish the day.

What your choices do

Human assignments emphasize trust, AI emphasizes reach, and Human + AI adds oversight. Story type makes AI a better or riskier fit for particular tasks. The Story outlook combines your assignments into live reach and trust estimates. Reach attracts readers, while trust helps retain them.

Moving through the board

Use the four desk tabs to move between Reporting, Editing, Packaging, and Audience. Each tab shows whether its five tasks are complete.

About the scoring model

The numerical effects are provisional game rules for playtesting. They are not empirical claims about the performance of people or AI. The final score combines traffic, repeat visitors, and subscribers.
