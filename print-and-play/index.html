Download the game

Players use the board to choose Human, AI, or Human + AI for 20 newsroom tasks. The facilitator introduces the story, calculates the results, and leads the discussion.

Download player board Download facilitator cards
Printing: Use US Letter paper in landscape orientation. Print one board for each player or team. The facilitator-card file contains 10 stories, with two cards per page.

Facilitate online

You can use the online facilitator guide instead of printing the facilitator cards.

Playtest note

The scoring and story effects are discussion tools, not empirical claims about people or AI.
