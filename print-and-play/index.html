Pen-and-paper playtest

A facilitator presents a randomly drawn news story. Players discuss the story and assign Human, AI, or Human + AI to each of 20 newsroom tasks. The facilitator then uses the selected story card to calculate and discuss the audience outcomes.

Download the pen-and-paper game (PDF)
The six-page file includes: one reusable player board and 10 facilitator cards, with two cards on each cut sheet. Print on US Letter paper in landscape orientation.

Before playing

These materials are still being tested. The scoring model and story effects are discussion tools, not empirical claims about the performance of people or AI.
